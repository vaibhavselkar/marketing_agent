## ⚠️ Important Considerations

### Realistic Expectations
1. **Start Small**: Don't expect 100 leads/day immediately
2. **Build Gradually**: Growth takes 3-6 months
3. **Quality over Quantity**: Better to have 10 qualified leads than 100 unqualified
4. **Consistency**: Daily effort compounds over time

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Inconsistent Posting**: Skipping days = lost momentum
2. **Poor Response Quality**: Generic responses = lost sales
3. **No Follow-up**: Single message = low conversion
4. **Ignoring Analytics**: Not tracking results = missed opportunities

### Success Factors
1. **Consistent Content**: Daily posting and engagement
2. **Quality Responses**: Personalized, helpful replies
3. **Follow-up Strategy**: Multiple touchpoints with leads
4. **Continuous Optimization**: Regularly improve based on data
